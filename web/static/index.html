    pals
    how it works Try out passive skills on this Pal: press change on a slot to pick one, and see what it does to the score for the goal. paltop also works out the best set of four, and where you can get each one. Passives are passed on by breeding, so a child can get them from either parent.

    passive slots

    how it works Which of this player's Pals to use for breeding. These effects come from partner skills and do not stack, so only the best one of each counts - the one with the most condenser stars.
    how it works Pick a boss to see which of this player's Pals do best against it. For each Pal paltop works out the damage it does each turn with its equipped skills, how many turns it lasts against the boss's skills, and from that the share of the boss's HP it takes on its own (100% = it wins alone). The best ones are also fought out 40 times in paltop's battle model for a win %. The game's data does not have the bosses' extra HP or the tower and raid levels, so pick those yourself: towers and raids start at the player's level, field bosses at their spawn level. Hard mode towers and raids are their own, stronger forms with more skills, and the save keeps hard mode tower wins apart from normal ones.
    how it works The team starts empty: press choose on a slot to pick a Pal for it, or fill empty slots with the best to let paltop fill the rest for the goal. In the choose list, the numbers in brackets are how much better (green) or worse (red) each Pal is than the one in the slot now, and compare opens both side by side. plan opens the passive planner for a Pal. Pick wishes - mounts, elements, roles - and paltop suggests a team that has each of them (one Pal can meet several), with the rest the best for the goal. count passives decides which passives the Pals are scored with: what they have, your plans, or their best possible set.

    traitpedia
    how it works Every trait (passive skill) a Pal can have, from the game's own data. Tier is the game's rank: I-III the usual good ones, rainbow and World Tree the best, and the bad ones below zero. Where: random can appear on any Pal you catch or breed, born with means every Pal of those species has it, and the surgery table implants it for gold - some also need an implant: an Implant is a key item you keep, a Disposable Implant is used up (the Ancient Relic Recycler gives them). Parents pass their traits on when breeding, so a trait a Pal on the server has can be bred over.
    quests
    how it works Every quest in the game and whether the picked player has done it, from their save: a check mark and grey text when it is done, in progress when they have taken it on. The story is in the order the game hands it out; side quests are grouped by who gives them. Hover a quest for what it asks, and who else on the server has done it. The save is read when the game saves, so a quest done just now can take a few minutes to show.
